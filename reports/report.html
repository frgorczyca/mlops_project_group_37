Answer:

For our project, we needed a pretrained transformer for classifying essays as either human- or AI-written, and so we decided to use the open-source Transformers library from HuggingFace. Specifically we used the bert-tiny model, which is a smaller, pre-trained BERT variant. Its smaller size allowed for faster training, whilst still maintaing good performance. Question 14

Upload 1 to 3 screenshots that show the experiments that you have done in W&B (or another experiment tracking service of your choice). This may include loss graphs, logged images, hyperparameter sweeps etc. You can take inspiration from this figure. Explain what metrics you are tracking and why they are important.

Recommended answer length: 200-300 words + 1 to 3 screenshots.

Example: As seen in the first image when have tracked ... and ... which both inform us about ... in our experiments. As seen in the second image we are also tracking ... and ...

Answer:

In the image, we can see 6 tracked metrics, although 10 charts are present, but limited by the page. Both loss, accuracy and precision are tracked. For training, the metrics are tracked both for each training step, and on each epoch, but for validation they are tracked only per epoch. These metrics provide crucial insights into model performance and training dynamics. Loss helps monitor how well the model is learning by measuring prediction errors, with lower values indicating better fit. Accuracy shows the overall correctness of predictions, while precision focuses on the reliability of positive predictions by measuring the ratio of true positives to all positive predictions. Tracking metrics at both step and epoch levels allows us to observe both fine-grained learning behavior and overall trends. Training metrics reveal how the model learns from the training data, while validation metrics indicate generalization ability on unseen data. Additional metrics such as recall (measuring the model's ability to find all positive cases), F1 score (balancing precision and recall), and AUROC (measuring discrimination ability across different thresholds) could be enabled via the config, however they quickly clutter the overview of charts. Focusing on key metrics helps maintain clarity and efficiency in the analysis process.
